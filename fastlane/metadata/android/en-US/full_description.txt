meditor is a Markdown, Typst and LaTeX editor with a live preview, PDF export,
tabs, session restore and a layout built for a finger rather than a mouse.

* Markdown, Typst and LaTeX with syntax highlighting and live preview.
* Paginated, print-faithful preview with PDF export.
* Open and save real files through the system document picker.
* Sessions are restored after the app is closed or killed.
* Documents stay on the device; there is no account and no telemetry.

The LaTeX preview downloads the TeX compiler from a configurable TeX Live
endpoint the first time it is used; all other features work offline.
